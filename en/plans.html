FAQ

Questions about our plans

Flexible contracts and à la carte services: let’s talk about what suits your home.

Ask for an estimate

What are your fees?

We offer two plans:

  • The guaranteed-income contract: based on your dates, your property and the average Airbnb occupancy rate, we calculate an income that is guaranteed and paid to you whether or not the apartment is booked. Our average margin is 15%.
  • The actual-income contract: we take a commission of 18% to 25% on the income your property generates, depending on the length of the rental. The more it is booked, the more you earn; if it is not booked, which is fairly rare, you receive nothing.

With actual income, we move nightly prices up or down with supply and demand on Airbnb to maximise bookings. You can set a minimum nightly price below which we will not go.

Compare the two plans

How do you calculate my income?

Your income depends on several factors: the property itself (address, number of beds…), the number of nights you want to entrust to us and the average Airbnb occupancy rate for that period. That rate is calculated by our dynamic pricing algorithm, from supply and demand on Airbnb. You then choose between a guaranteed income and your actual income.

How much can I expect to earn?

Your return depends on many things: location, size, quality, the season… We combine analysis tools with our knowledge of the local market to estimate your earnings. After a detailed assessment of your property and your goals, we give you a realistic, transparent projection of your income. Ask for your estimate.

How is my nightly price set?

We use analysis tools that follow market trends, demand, local events and seasonality in real time. We combine them with our knowledge of the area and of your property to set a competitive price that aims for both good occupancy and good income.

Why does my price change during the year?

Demand moves with the seasons, school holidays, events and travel trends. With dynamic pricing, the price rises when demand is high and adjusts to attract bookings in quieter periods. This helps keep occupancy high all year while maximising your income.

Can I choose only some services?

Yes. Every owner’s needs are different: you can hand us full management of your property, or only maintenance, cleaning or bookings.
