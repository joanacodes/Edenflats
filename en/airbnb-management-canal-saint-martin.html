Letting a furnished home along the Canal Saint-Martin

Opened in 1825, the Canal Saint-Martin runs in the open air through the 10th arrondissement, from the Bassin de la Villette to Place de la République. Its plane-tree-lined quays, Quai de Valmy and Quai de Jemmapes, are lined with cafés, designer shops and the Hôtel du Nord, made famous by Marcel Carné’s film.

Gare du Nord and Gare de l’Est are a few minutes’ walk away: a convenient arrival point for guests coming by train from London, Brussels or Germany, and RER B runs from Gare du Nord to Charles de Gaulle airport.

Letting in Paris: the essentials

90 days a yearThe maximum for letting your main residence as furnished tourist accommodation in Paris, since 1 January 2025.
Registration numberThe property must be declared to the City; its number appears on every listing.
Change of useLetting a second home for short stays requires City authorisation, usually with compensation.
Landlord’s consentRenting? You need your landlord’s written consent to sublet.
