Plans and income

What are your fees?

We offer two plans:

  • The guaranteed-income contract: based on your dates, your property and the average Airbnb occupancy rate, we calculate an income that is guaranteed and paid to you whether or not the apartment is booked. Our average margin is 15%.
  • The actual-income contract: we take a commission of 18% to 25% on the income your property generates, depending on the length of the rental. The more it is booked, the more you earn; if it is not booked, which is fairly rare, you receive nothing.

With actual income, we move nightly prices up or down with supply and demand on Airbnb to maximise bookings. You can set a minimum nightly price below which we will not go.

Compare the two plans

How do you calculate my income?

Your income depends on several factors: the property itself (address, number of beds…), the number of nights you want to entrust to us and the average Airbnb occupancy rate for that period. That rate is calculated by our dynamic pricing algorithm, from supply and demand on Airbnb. You then choose between a guaranteed income and your actual income.

How much can I expect to earn?

Your return depends on many things: location, size, quality, the season… We combine analysis tools with our knowledge of the local market to estimate your earnings. After a detailed assessment of your property and your goals, we give you a realistic, transparent projection of your income. Ask for your estimate.

How is my nightly price set?

We use analysis tools that follow market trends, demand, local events and seasonality in real time. We combine them with our knowledge of the area and of your property to set a competitive price that aims for both good occupancy and good income.

Why does my price change during the year?

Demand moves with the seasons, school holidays, events and travel trends. With dynamic pricing, the price rises when demand is high and adjusts to attract bookings in quieter periods. This helps keep occupancy high all year while maximising your income.

Can I choose only some services?

Yes. Every owner’s needs are different: you can hand us full management of your property, or only maintenance, cleaning or bookings.

Your listing and Airbnb account

I don’t have an Airbnb account. What do I need to do?

Nothing: you don’t need to create a profile. Our team handles the whole listing set-up and gives you tips to build trust with future guests.

Can I keep my Airbnb host account?

Yes. If you have already built your profile and reviews on Airbnb, you keep them: we manage your bookings from that account, taking care to maintain and improve your reputation.

Can I still use my account and see my bookings?

Yes. You keep full access to your Airbnb account: bookings, guest reviews and income, in real time. To avoid conflicts and double bookings, just let us know about any change you want to make.

Which platforms is my listing published on?

On Airbnb, and also on Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia and other platforms. Each one attracts different travellers: being on several channels widens your audience and increases your chances of bookings all year.

Who takes the photos for my listing?

We do. First impressions matter: we photograph your property ourselves to show its best angles and build guests’ trust.

Which languages is my listing written in?

Usually French and English, to reach international travellers.

Guests and bookings

Do you screen guests?

Yes. A dedicated team handles only guest screening, communication and guest management, so your property is let to people you can trust. We look at each guest’s history and reviews, and often talk with them to understand the purpose of their stay. An algorithm we developed also helps us select guests against specific criteria:

  • we ask for their age and the reason for the booking, insisting with guests who have no reviews;
  • we favour guests with positive reviews and verified profiles;
  • we make bookings easier for families;
  • an ID is required to book: every guest’s identity is checked.
How are my bookings managed?

Our system syncs automatically with every platform your listing is on, so calendars are up to date in real time and double bookings are avoided. Our team stays in touch with guests, answers their questions and sends them everything they need before arrival. For check-in, we agree the time with them and make sure the apartment is spotless. If anything unexpected happens, we are available to sort it out quickly.

What happens when guests cancel?

We use the platforms’ strict cancellation policies to limit last-minute cancellations. If a booking is cancelled, we put the dates back online straight away and promote them to find new guests; because we are on several platforms, this is often possible at short notice.

Can I visit my property while a guest is staying?

It is your home, but guests’ privacy matters: please let us know before any visit during a stay. If it is urgent, we arrange the visit with the guest so that it disturbs them as little as possible.

Keys, cleaning and upkeep

How are the keys handled?

We look after the key handover for the whole rental period. We need two sets: the day before the rental, you drop the first one at one of our partner cafés; the second stays in the apartment, on the kitchen worktop. During the rental, one set goes to our cleaning provider and the other to the guests.

If you prefer, we can also open remotely, with a smart lock or a key box.

What does the cleaning include?

Cleaning includes:

  • a deep clean of every room;
  • washing and ironing sheets, towels and household linen;
  • restocking consumables: shampoo, shower gel, toilet paper;
  • airing the apartment;
  • checking that appliances and equipment work properly.

Every new guest walks into a fresh, clean and welcoming home. More on property management.

How do damage claims work?

If a guest causes damage, we document it with photos, a detailed description and, where needed, repair quotes. We then file the claim with the platform concerned or the guest’s insurer. If no amicable agreement is possible, we handle the whole procedure.

What are the risks of short-term letting?

Short-term letting carries risks: accidental damage, disputes with guests or empty periods. We limit them by screening guests, maintaining the property regularly and adjusting prices to maximise occupancy. We also advise all our clients to take out insurance suited to short-term rentals.

Regulations

What are the rules for Airbnb in France?

The rules depend on the town and on your situation. The main ones:

  • Main residence: you can let it as furnished tourist accommodation for up to 120 days a year. Towns can lower this cap: in Paris it has been 90 days since 1 January 2025.
  • Registration: in towns that require it, including Paris, the property must be declared to the town hall and its registration number shown on every listing.
  • Second home or buy-to-let: in Paris, the inner suburbs and other towns, letting it repeatedly for short stays generally requires a change-of-use authorisation.
  • Tenants: you need your landlord’s written consent to sublet.

These rules change: check the ones that apply to your property (see also the Paris rules) with your town hall or on service-public.gouv.fr (in French) before you start letting it.
