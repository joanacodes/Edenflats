FAQ

Questions about guests

Do you screen guests?

Yes. A dedicated team handles only guest screening, communication and guest management, so your property is let to people you can trust. We look at each guest’s history and reviews, and often talk with them to understand the purpose of their stay. An algorithm we developed also helps us select guests against specific criteria:

  • we ask for their age and the reason for the booking, insisting with guests who have no reviews;
  • we favour guests with positive reviews and verified profiles;
  • we make bookings easier for families;
  • an ID is required to book: every guest’s identity is checked.
How are my bookings managed?

Our system syncs automatically with every platform your listing is on, so calendars are up to date in real time and double bookings are avoided. Our team stays in touch with guests, answers their questions and sends them everything they need before arrival. For check-in, we agree the time with them and make sure the apartment is spotless. If anything unexpected happens, we are available to sort it out quickly.

Can I still use my account and see my bookings?

Yes. You keep full access to your Airbnb account: bookings, guest reviews and income, in real time. To avoid conflicts and double bookings, just let us know about any change you want to make.

What happens when guests cancel?

We use the platforms’ strict cancellation policies to limit last-minute cancellations. If a booking is cancelled, we put the dates back online straight away and promote them to find new guests; because we are on several platforms, this is often possible at short notice.

How are the keys handled?

We look after the key handover for the whole rental period. We need two sets: the day before the rental, you drop the first one at one of our partner cafés; the second stays in the apartment, on the kitchen worktop. During the rental, one set goes to our cleaning provider and the other to the guests.

If you prefer, we can also open remotely, with a smart lock or a key box.
