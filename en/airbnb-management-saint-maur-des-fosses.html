Letting a furnished home in Saint-Maur-des-Fossés

Saint-Maur-des-Fossés fills almost an entire loop of the Marne, in Val-de-Marne. RER A serves several stations, including Saint-Maur–Créteil, Le Parc de Saint-Maur and La Varenne–Chennevières, and runs to Nation, Châtelet–Les Halles and La Défense without changing.

Each neighbourhood has its own character, from La Varenne to the Vieux Saint-Maur by way of Le Parc and Champignol: houses with gardens, quiet apartments, walks along the Marne. A good listing shows what the home does best: the station on foot, the river or a garden.

Your home in Saint-Maur-des-Fossés, managed by EdenFlats

We know the east of Paris well: our head office is in Sucy-en-Brie, one RER stop from La Varenne. Cleaning, linen and small repairs are organised close to your home.

We handle guest management (listing, screening, keys, support) and property management (cleaning, linen, upkeep), with a guaranteed or an actual income. Owners’ questions are answered in our FAQ.
