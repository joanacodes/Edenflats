Letting a furnished home near the Louvre

The 1st arrondissement stretches from the Louvre and the Tuileries Garden to Les Halles. It includes the Palais-Royal garden and its Buren columns, the Place Vendôme, the Bourse de Commerce – Pinault Collection and, at the tip of the Île de la Cité, the Pont Neuf.

Guests choose the 1st to do everything on foot, from the Louvre to Notre-Dame. Buildings are old and apartments often compact, so every square metre has to show well in the photos. At Châtelet–Les Halles, RER lines A, B and D run to La Défense, Disneyland Paris, Charles de Gaulle airport and the Stade de France.

Your home near the Louvre, managed by EdenFlats

In a compact apartment, photos make the booking: we take them ourselves and, where needed, advise you on decoration so that every square metre counts.

Regulations

Letting in Paris: the essentials

90 days a yearThe maximum for letting your main residence as furnished tourist accommodation in Paris, since 1 January 2025.
Registration numberThe property must be declared to the City; its number appears on every listing.
Change of useLetting a second home for short stays requires City authorisation, usually with compensation.
Landlord’s consentRenting? You need your landlord’s written consent to sublet.

These rules change: check them before letting on paris.fr (in French).
