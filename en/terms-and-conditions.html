Article 1 – Purpose and scope

These terms and conditions govern the contractual relationship between EDENEL PATRIMOINE GESTION RESEAUX & SERVICES (EPGRS), a French simplified joint-stock company (SAS) whose registered office is at 14 rue de Champigny, 94370 Sucy-en-Brie, France, SIRET 104 632 591 00012, which operates the EdenFlats brand (“EdenFlats”), and any person who entrusts it with concierge services for a home let on a short or mid-term basis (“the Client”).

The management agreement or the quote accepted by the Client details and supplements these terms; where they conflict, it prevails.

Article 2 – Services

EdenFlats offers in particular the following services:

  • key handover and collection, guest welcome (check-in and check-out);
  • professional cleaning;
  • online booking management;
  • communication with guests;
  • laundry and linen supply;
  • basic maintenance and emergency handling.

The services are described on www.edenflats.com, in particular on the Guest management, Property management and Plans pages, and set out in the agreement or quote given to the Client.

Article 3 – Eligibility

To use the services, the Client must own or lawfully manage a property let on a short-term basis. The Client provides the information needed to perform the services: address of the home, access, calendar, specific instructions, etc.

The Client ensures that the rules applying to the letting of the home are complied with (registration with the city, registration number, number of nights allowed, building rules). Our FAQ sums up the rules in force in Paris.

Article 4 – Prices and payment

Prices are given in a personalised quote or on the website ( Plans page), in euros including all taxes. Services can be paid by bank transfer, by card or by direct debit, if agreed.

Any service that has started is due in full. Extra charges may apply to specific or urgent requests; the Client is told about them before the work is done.

If the Client is a business, any late payment automatically incurs penalties at three times the French statutory interest rate and a fixed recovery fee of €40 (article L441-10 of the French Commercial Code).

Article 5 – Booking and cancelling services

Any service request must be confirmed at least [number] hours in advance. The Client may cancel free of charge up to [number] hours before the service; after that, the full amount is due.

Article 6 – Right of withdrawal

If the Client is a consumer and the contract was concluded at a distance (by phone, email or WhatsApp, for example) or off-premises, the Client has fourteen days from the conclusion of the contract to withdraw, without giving any reason, by an unambiguous statement sent to EdenFlats, for example by email to edenflats.paris@gmail.com (articles L221-18 et seq. of the French Consumer Code).

If the Client expressly asks for the service to start before the end of this period, the Client remains liable for the amount corresponding to the service provided until withdrawal. A service fully performed before the end of the period, at the Client’s request, can no longer be withdrawn from.

Article 7 – EdenFlats’ commitments

EdenFlats undertakes to perform the services diligently, professionally and to current quality standards. If a service cannot be performed (force majeure, accident, etc.), EdenFlats undertakes to inform the Client as soon as possible.

Article 8 – Liability

EdenFlats cannot be held liable for:

  • damage caused by guests;
  • theft or damage occurring in its absence;
  • loss of income due to cancellations beyond its management.

If guests cause damage, EdenFlats helps the Client with the claim to the booking platform. The Client remains solely responsible for the tax and legal declarations relating to the letting of the property.

Article 9 – Insurance

The Client undertakes to hold home insurance covering short-term letting. EdenFlats holds professional liability insurance (insurer and policy number to be completed).

Article 10 – Personal data

The Client’s personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as described in our privacy policy. The Client may at any time ask to access, correct or delete their data by writing to edenflats.paris@gmail.com.

Article 11 – Consumer mediation

If the Client is a consumer, they may refer a dispute with EdenFlats to a consumer mediator free of charge, after first sending a written complaint to EdenFlats (articles L612-1 et seq. of the French Consumer Code). Mediator: mediator name, address and website to be completed.

Article 12 – Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by French law. In the event of a dispute, an amicable solution will be sought first.

Failing that, if the Client is a business, the dispute will be brought before the competent courts for the area of EdenFlats’ registered office, in Sucy-en-Brie. If the Client is a consumer, they may choose to bring the matter before any court with territorial jurisdiction or the court for the place where they lived when the contract was concluded or when the damage occurred.
