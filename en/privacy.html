Privacy and cookie policy

This website has no forms, no cookies and no audience measurement tools. Here is what happens when you contact us.

Data controller

EDENEL PATRIMOINE GESTION RESEAUX & SERVICES (EPGRS), 14 rue de Champigny, 94370 Sucy-en-Brie, France, which operates the EdenFlats brand. Contact: edenflats.paris@gmail.com.

Data collected

The website does not collect any data itself: it has no forms and sets no cookies or trackers. Fonts are hosted on the site, with no call to a third-party service.

When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we use your contact details and your message to reply and, if we work together, to prepare and perform our contract.

Retention

Exchanges with people who do not become clients are kept for at most three years after the last contact. Client data is kept for the duration of the contract, then for as long as our legal obligations require, notably accounting.

Third-party services

  • WhatsApp is a service of WhatsApp Ireland Limited: if you message us through it, its own privacy policy applies.
  • The website host, GitHub, Inc., may record visitors’ IP addresses in its technical logs, for security purposes.
  • Links to Instagram, Facebook and our booking page lead to third-party sites, which apply their own rules.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing and request its portability, by writing to edenflats.paris@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).
