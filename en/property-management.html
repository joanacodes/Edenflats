  • Sheets, towels and household linen washed and ironed
  • Consumables restocked: shampoo, shower gel, toilet paper

Disputes and damage

If a problem arises, we handle it

If a disagreement arises, we mediate, protecting your interests while seeking a fair settlement for everyone.

If a guest causes damage, we document it with photos, a detailed description and, where needed, repair quotes, then file the claim with the platform or the guest’s insurer.

We advise all our clients to take out insurance suited to short-term rentals.

What does the cleaning include?

Cleaning includes:

  • a deep clean of every room;
  • washing and ironing sheets, towels and household linen;
  • restocking consumables: shampoo, shower gel, toilet paper;
  • airing the apartment;
  • checking that appliances and equipment work properly.

Every new guest walks into a fresh, clean and welcoming home. More on property management.

How do damage claims work?

If a guest causes damage, we document it with photos, a detailed description and, where needed, repair quotes. We then file the claim with the platform concerned or the guest’s insurer. If no amicable agreement is possible, we handle the whole procedure.

Can I visit my property while a guest is staying?

It is your home, but guests’ privacy matters: please let us know before any visit during a stay. If it is urgent, we arrange the visit with the guest so that it disturbs them as little as possible.

What are the risks of short-term letting?

Short-term letting carries risks: accidental damage, disputes with guests or empty periods. We limit them by screening guests, maintaining the property regularly and adjusting prices to maximise occupancy. We also advise all our clients to take out insurance suited to short-term rentals.

Can I choose only some services?

Yes. Every owner’s needs are different: you can hand us full management of your property, or only maintenance, cleaning or bookings.
