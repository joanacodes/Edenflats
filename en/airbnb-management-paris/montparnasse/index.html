Letting a furnished home in Montparnasse

The area made its name in the 1920s with the painters and writers who met at La Coupole, Le Dôme, La Rotonde and Le Select, on Boulevard du Montparnasse. Today it centres on the Tour Montparnasse and the station, with the Montparnasse cemetery and, at Denfert-Rochereau, the entrance to the Catacombs.

Gare Montparnasse links Paris to Brittany and the south-west by TGV: many business travellers look for a practical apartment a few minutes from the train, for a few nights or several weeks on assignment.

Letting in Paris: the essentials

90 days a yearThe maximum for letting your main residence as furnished tourist accommodation in Paris, since 1 January 2025.
Registration numberThe property must be declared to the City; its number appears on every listing.
Change of useLetting a second home for short stays requires City authorisation, usually with compensation.
Landlord’s consentRenting? You need your landlord’s written consent to sublet.
