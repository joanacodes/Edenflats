Letting a furnished home in Montmartre

Montmartre is the hill of the 18th arrondissement, crowned by the Sacré-Cœur basilica. Around the Place du Tertre and its painters, cobbled streets lead down to Abbesses, the Musée de Montmartre and the Clos Montmartre vineyard, harvested every autumn during the Fête des Vendanges.

It is a neighbourhood of couples and short stays, where the view and the charm of an artist’s flat matter as much as floor space. Guests climb the hill on foot, by its many stairs, or take the funicular: a route worth planning with luggage.

Letting in Paris: the essentials

90 days a yearThe maximum for letting your main residence as furnished tourist accommodation in Paris, since 1 January 2025.
Registration numberThe property must be declared to the City; its number appears on every listing.
Change of useLetting a second home for short stays requires City authorisation, usually with compensation.
Landlord’s consentRenting? You need your landlord’s written consent to sublet.
